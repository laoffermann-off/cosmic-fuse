Cosmic Physics, Pure Focus

Easy to learn in seconds, endlessly replayable. Aim carefully, manage limited orbital space, and climb the cosmic evolution chain.

Merge identical bodies to evolve from tiny Asteroids through Moon, Mercury, Mars, Earth, Neptune, Saturn, Jupiter, the Sun, and finally a Black Hole.

Built natively with SwiftUI and SpriteKit for ultra-responsive controls, realistic rolling collisions, and crisp procedural planetary art.

Drop Planets. Merge Up to a Black Hole.

A physics-based planet merge game for iPhone. Line up your drops, combine matching celestial bodies, and try not to overflow the container.

Download on the App Store iOS 17.6+ • No Ads • Works Offline

How It Works

Simple rules, tight space. When two identical bodies touch, they fuse into the next larger stage and award points.

10 Stages

Start with Asteroids and Moons and merge your way up through Mercury, Mars, Earth, Neptune, Saturn, Jupiter, the Sun, and finally a Black Hole.

Native iOS Physics

Built with SwiftUI and SpriteKit with 120 Hz ProMotion support on compatible iPhones for smooth motion and responsive drops.

Offline & Ad-Free

No ads, no sign-ups, and no internet connection needed. High scores and sound settings stay locally on your device.

Merge Order

1. Asteroid 2. Moon 3. Mercury 4. Mars 5. Earth 6. Neptune 7. Saturn 8. Jupiter 9. Sun 10. Black Hole

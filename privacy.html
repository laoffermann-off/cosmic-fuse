Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Cosmic Fuse is an iOS game developed by Leon-André Offermann. The app works completely offline and does not collect, track, or transmit personal data.

1. No Tracking or Third-Party SDKs

Cosmic Fuse does not include advertising networks, analytics tools, crash trackers, or external server connections. No personal data, device identifiers, or usage statistics are collected or shared.

2. Local Storage on Your Device

The app only stores two basic values locally on your iPhone using Apple's UserDefaults:

  • Your local high score.
  • Your sound mute/unmute setting.

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you remove the app.

3. App Store & Website Hosting

Downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical connection data (such as IP addresses) in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Contact

If you send an email to support, your address and message are used solely to reply to your request.

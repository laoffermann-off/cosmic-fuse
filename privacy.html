Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Cosmic Fuze is developed by Leon-André Offermann. Your privacy is respected by design: the app works completely offline and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. Data Collection & Third-Party Services

Cosmic Fuze does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash-reporting SDKs, or external servers. No personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage statistics are collected or shared with third parties.

2. Local Storage on Your Device

To provide core game functionality, the app stores minimal preferences locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage:

  • Your personal local high score.
  • Your sound mute/unmute preference.

This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the app.

3. Apple App Store & GitHub Pages

When you download Cosmic Fuze from the Apple App Store, Apple may process technical data in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. When you visit this support website hosted via GitHub Pages, GitHub may process standard server log information (such as IP addresses) for security and delivery purposes in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Contact & Data Controller

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will only be used to respond to your inquiry.
